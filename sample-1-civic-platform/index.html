Formatted Statutory Notice Preview 
Subject: Formal Statutory Grievance under Rule 4(5), Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 – Order #[ORDER-ID]

To: The Grievance Officer, [Company Name]

Dear Grievance Officer,

I am lodging a formal statutory complaint regarding Order #[ORDER-ID] for ₹[Amount], which remains unresolved due to automated chatbot deflection on your platform.

Under Rule 4(5) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, you are legally obligated to acknowledge this complaint within 48 hours with a unique tracking docket and resolve it within one month. Failing timely resolution, I will escalate this record to the National Consumer Helpline (1915) and the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

Yours sincerely,
[Your Name]
01

Level 1: Consumer VOICE Desk & NCH (1915)

48-Hr Acknowledgment

First email the company’s published Grievance Officer. If unanswered after 48 hours, register your docket on the National Consumer Helpline (Dial 1915 / consumerhelpline.gov.in) or request free pre-litigation review from Consumer VOICE’s VCO Advisory Desk.

Toll-Free: 1915 Desk: info@consumer-voice.org
02

Level 2: Sector Regulators (RBI CMS & CCPA)

Statutory Enforcement

For unauthorized UPI debits, failed refunds, or forced auto-mandates unresolved after 30 days, file on the RBI Ombudsman Portal (cms.rbi.org.in). For dark patterns (basket sneaking, drip pricing, subscription traps), report directly to the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA).

03

Level 3: Consumer Commission via e-Daakhil

Binding Adjudication

File a formal consumer complaint online from home at edaakhil.nic.in—no advocate required. Attach your invoice, timestamped screenshots of the chatbot deflection loop, and your unanswered statutory notice to claim refund plus compensation.
