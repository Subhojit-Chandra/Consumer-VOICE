Under Rule 4(5) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, you are legally obligated to acknowledge this complaint within 48 hours with a unique tracking docket and resolve it within one month. Level 1: Pre-Litigation Mediation

48-Hr Rule

Email the platform’s Grievance Officer first. If unresolved after 48 hours, log a docket with the National Consumer Helpline (1915) or seek pre-litigation guidance from the Consumer VOICE Legal Guidance Desk.

NCH Toll-Free: 1915 info@consumer-voice.org
02

Level 2: Sector Regulators

Statutory Enforcement

For unauthorized UPI debits or failed bank refunds unresolved after 30 days, file at the RBI Ombudsman (cms.rbi.org.in). For drip pricing, basket sneaking, or subscription traps, submit evidence to the CCPA Dark Pattern Portal.

03

Level 3: Consumer Commission

e-Daakhil Portal

File a formal complaint online from home via the e-Daakhil Online Portal (edaakhil.nic.in) without hiring an advocate. Attach your invoice, chatbot screenshots, and unanswered statutory notice to claim refund and compensation.
