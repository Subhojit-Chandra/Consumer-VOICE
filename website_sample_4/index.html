2. The 3-Tier Statutory Escalation Ladder

Tier 01 • Pre-Litigation Mediation Days 1–15

Consumer VOICE VCO Desk & NCH (1915)

Log your unanswered Grievance Officer notice with the National Consumer Helpline (dial 1915 or consumerhelpline.gov.in) and forward your evidence trail to Consumer VOICE (info@consumer-voice.org) for VCO institutional mediation.

Tier 02 • Sectoral & Enforcement Regulators Days 15–30

RBI Ombudsman (CMS) / CCPA / FSSAI

For unauthorized UPI debits or bank delays, file on cms.rbi.org.in. For systemic dark patterns or misleading ads, submit evidence for class-action enforcement under Sec. 18 CPA 2019 (CCPA).

Tier 03 • Binding Judicial Redressal Formal Adjudication

e-Daakhil Consumer Disputes Commission

File directly online at edaakhil.nic.in without needing a lawyer. Zero court filing fee applies for claims up to ₹5 lakh; claim refund, interest, and compensation for harassment.

The Consumer VOICE Covenant

100% Zero-Corporate-Sponsorship & Zero-Advertisement Charter

Born out of Delhi University in 1983, Consumer VOICE’s credibility rests on uncompromising independence. Our laboratory scores and editorial verdicts can never be influenced by corporate funds.

RULE 01

Zero Commercial Ads

Neither VOICE Magazine nor our website accepts brand advertisements, sponsored reviews, or affiliate commissions.

RULE 02

Anonymous Retail Sampling

Every product tested in our NABL lab program is bought anonymously off retail shelves—never accepted from manufacturers.

RULE 03

Public-Interest Funding

Funded strictly by citizen subscriptions, 80G donations, and audited public-interest grants (Consumer Welfare Fund, Consumers International).
